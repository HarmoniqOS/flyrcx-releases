# Fly RC-X: fixes and updates

Fly RC-X updates itself. This page lists what changed in each version.

## 0.1.1

- **Callout text moved out of the way.** Maneuver names over RealFlight now appear at the bottom center of the screen instead of the top, where the airplane usually is. The text is also smaller by default.
- **New callout options on the Fly screen.** Choose the text size (Small, Medium, Large) or turn the text off, and turn the spoken callouts on or off. Your choices are remembered, and drills use them too.
- **Home button on every screen.** A HOME button now sits in the top-left corner on every screen, so you never have to back out through menus. Clicking the Fly RC-X logo also goes home.
- **Home screen titles no longer cut off.** On some screen sizes the titles of the Train, Debrief and Library cards were clipped at the top. Fixed.

## 0.1.0

- First beta release.
- **Rifle Roll Trainer on the home screen.** Arm the roll indicator in one click from the home screen. The settings link opens the full trainer options.
- **Loop drill counts round loops.** Smooth, big loops are detected and scored, and a rep that doesn't count tells you why.
